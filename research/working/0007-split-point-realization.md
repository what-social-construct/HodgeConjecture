## What was constructed

For a field `k` and finite set `I`, `SplitPointGeometry` constructs
`Z_I = Spec(k^I)`, its finite, étale, proper structure map, and the open and
closed component inclusion `s_i : Spec k → Z_I`. The span
`Z_I ← Spec k → Z_I`, with both legs `s_i`, is an actual scheme
correspondence. Pulling a monomorphism back along itself yields an
isomorphism of its self-composition with the same span. Its basis element
`π_i` in the free finite-étale span algebra is therefore idempotent.

For two labels, `SplitPointCorrespondence` separately defines rational
coefficient cycles `Q^2` and the convolution algebra `M_2(Q)`. In rows-output,
columns-input convention, the first point correspondence is `Π=E₀₀`.
The marked source, residual map, and coordinate change are explicit:

```text
A = Q × Q,   ε(a,b) = a,   q = (1,0),
E = ker ε = {0} × Q,       θ(0,b) = b,
q−1 = (0,−1),             θ(q−1) = −1,
T(a,b) = (a,b−a),         ρ(C) = T C T⁻¹.
```

Lean proves that `T` is precisely the source's constant-unit decomposition
followed by residual evaluation, not an arbitrary target-dependent shear.
It computes `ρ(E₀₀)=P₋₁`. This constructs the comparison square, and
surjectivity of `θ` yields the projector lift and the algebra-map equality
`ρ ∘ φ_Π = φ_(P₋₁)` through the existing universal-idempotent machinery.
The target class is the output of the chosen source, not an arbitrary Hodge
class supplied in advance.

## Interface obstruction found by the test

The previous selector comparison required an `AlgebraProjectorExtension`
with a unital map from the entire ambient correspondence algebra to the
commutative sheet algebra. This cannot work for the full two-point matrix
algebra. `MatrixRestrictionObstruction.no_ringHom_to_commutative` proves the
obstruction even for unital ring maps to any nontrivial commutative ring.
The proof uses the matrix-unit identities, not an unproved simplicity theorem.

`MarkedProjectorActionComparison M ρ Π` is now the canonical core. Its only
fields are the residual linear map and its action square. Idempotence of `Π`
is an explicit hypothesis of its universal representation and lift methods.
The original `MarkedSelectorActionComparison` and optional presented variant
extend that core and delegate their consequences; no comparison fields or
proofs are duplicated. The existing Galois coverage interface still carries
its extra ambient restriction: it remains a stronger specialized route.

## Next arrow at this checkpoint

The actual span and the coefficient matrix are not yet related by a Lean map.
In particular, the free span algebra does not impose disjoint-union
additivity or empty-span vanishing. Its basis projector is not definitionally
the matrix unit, and pairwise orthogonality of point projectors in a Chow or
matrix algebra cannot simply be inferred inside that free algebra.

The next bounded construction is an additive, multiplicative realization for
finite split point spans:

```text
geometric finite split spans
    → rational point-cycle correspondences
    → matrices on Q^I,
```

with the `i`-diagonal point sent to `E_ii`, disjoint union sent to sum, empty
span sent to zero, and fiber-product composition sent to matrix
multiplication. Start with explicitly split carriers and prove these laws;
do not assume a `FiniteEtaleAlgebraModel` for arbitrary spans as a substitute.
Over a general field, finite-étale carriers need not split. Extending to all
such carriers needs a degree realization, or splitting after base change
with descent compatibility, as additional work.

For the Chow interpretation, rational equivalence in dimension zero is
generated by divisors of functions on integral curves; a finite split scheme
has no such curves. Thus its rational zero-cycle group has the point basis.
This is an elementary specialization of [Stacks, Definition 42.19.1,
Tag 02RW](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/02RW). The matrix composition
interpretation uses [Stacks, Lemma 45.3.1,
Tag 0FG0](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0FG0), with variance fixed
explicitly. These source identifications have not yet been encoded as a
scheme Chow comparison or a Betti cycle-class map in Lean.
